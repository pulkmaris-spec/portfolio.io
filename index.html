About Me

Hi! I'm Maris, a digital product designer! With over 5 years of experience in payments and fintech UX, I design user-first flows for those moments where products ask the most of their user (payments, verification, sign-up, backoffice tools and dashboards) and create seamless experiences. With a combined experience of B2B and B2C products, I have faced and enjoyed problems with constraints because that's where design decisions actually matter.

Fun facts about me: I love to read, draw, I listen to rock/metal music and I am a proud owner of a shelter rescued dog.

How I Work

01

Discover

Dig into the problem space — talk to users, review data, and align with stakeholders on what actually needs solving.

02

Define

Frame the problem clearly, set success metrics, and scope the smallest version worth building.

03

Design

Explore broadly, then narrow fast. Prototype in low fidelity, test assumptions, and refine with real feedback.

04

Deliver

Partner closely with engineering to ship, measure the impact, and iterate based on what we learn.
